A modern waterproofing material that protects underground and aboveground structures from water and moisture. The profiled surface lets water flow freely and equalizes pressure, giving long-term protection to foundations, walls, and other structural elements.

Resistant to mechanical stress, corrosion, and environmental exposure; widely used in buildings, basements, and road/infrastructure projects.
