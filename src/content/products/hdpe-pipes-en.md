HDPE pipes are corrosion-resistant and suitable for both underground and aboveground installation. A smooth inner surface maintains water flow and reduces hydraulic loss, and pipe sections are joined by thermal welding for a high-strength connection.

Full wall-thickness and weight tables across all SDR/PN classes and diameters are available in the downloadable catalogue.
